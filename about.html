Our Story

Built on referrals, not billboards

Art Gallegos Construction started the way most good trade businesses do — one job at a time, for neighbors who told other neighbors. There was never a sales team. There was just a guy who did careful work, cleaned up after himself, and answered the phone when someone called back a year later.

That's still how it runs. Art quotes the work, Art does the work, and Art is the one you talk to if something isn't right. It keeps the business smaller than it could be. It also keeps the standard exactly where he wants it.

Roofing, painting, and repair aren't three separate businesses here — they're the three things that come up over and over in the same house. Knowing all three means we can tell you when a paint problem is really a water problem, and fix the cause instead of the symptom.
