Service 01

Roofing

A roof is the one part of your house that fails quietly. By the time you see a stain on the ceiling, the damage underneath has usually been building for a while. We look at the whole system — decking, underlayment, flashing, ventilation — not just the shingles you can see from the driveway.

  • Full roof replacement (asphalt shingle, metal, and tile)
  • Leak diagnosis and targeted repair
  • Storm and hail damage assessment
  • Flashing, valley, and vent boot replacement
  • Gutter and fascia repair
  • Documentation and photos for insurance claims

Service 02

Painting

Ninety percent of a paint job is what happens before the first coat. Scraping, sanding, patching, caulking, priming — that's the part that decides whether your house looks good for two years or ten. We don't skip it, and we'll show you exactly what we're doing at each stage.

  • Interior painting — walls, ceilings, trim, doors, cabinets
  • Exterior painting — siding, stucco, trim, fascia, soffits
  • Full surface prep: scrape, sand, patch, caulk, prime
  • Drywall and stucco patching before paint
  • Color consultation and sample boards
  • Floors, fixtures, and landscaping masked and protected
Get a painting estimate

Service 03

Residential Home Repair

This is the work Art has been doing the longest. Doors that stick, drywall that cracked, a bathroom that needs to be put back together, siding that took a hit — the jobs that are too small for a big contractor and too involved for a handyman. If it's part of your house, it's worth asking about.

  • Drywall repair, texture matching, and finishing
  • Trim, molding, doors, and window casing
  • Kitchen and bathroom repair and refresh
  • Siding, soffit, and exterior wood replacement
  • Water damage repair and rot remediation
  • Small remodels and punch-list work
