Privacy Policy

Last updated: add date

What we collect

When you submit the estimate form on this site, we collect the information you choose to give us: your name, phone number, email address, property address, the service you're interested in, and your description of the project.

How we use it

We use your information for exactly one purpose: to respond to your request and, if you hire us, to carry out the work. We use it to contact you about your estimate, schedule a visit, and follow up on the project.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information.
  • We don't rent or trade your information to marketers.
  • We don't add you to a mailing list without you asking.

Who else sees it

Form submissions are processed and stored by our website host. We may share limited details with suppliers or insurance adjusters when it is necessary to complete work you have hired us to do. We may also disclose information where required by law.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking. Basic, anonymous traffic statistics may be collected by our hosting provider.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to tell you what information we have about you, correct it, or delete it. Contact us using the details on the contact page and we'll take care of it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at info@artgallegosconstruction.com.
